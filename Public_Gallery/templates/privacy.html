Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we securely collect the following information:

  • Account Data: Email addresses and usernames required for authentication.
  • User Generated Content: Photos, videos, and text content uploaded to your vault.
  • Engagement Data: Likes, tips, and comments to calculate algorithmic feed rankings.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used to improve your experience. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Uploaded media is securely routed through our CDN (Cloudinary) partners for fast delivery.

3. Data Protection & Security

We employ military-grade security including CSRF tokens, strict XSS escaping, and IP-level rate limiting to protect your account from malicious attacks and unauthorized access.

4. Account Deletion

You have the absolute right to be forgotten. You can request account deletion from your Settings Hub. Upon deletion, all your media, chats, and interactions are permanently erased from our servers within 7 days.

For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact the Administration.
